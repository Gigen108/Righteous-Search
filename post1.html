Starting Small

April 12, 2026

I wanted a website that I could understand fully. No complicated system, no theme engine, no extra tools. Just files I can open and edit myself.

Starting small makes it easier to keep writing. It also makes it easier to publish, fix, and redesign later.

This site is my beginning. I can always add more pages and more posts as I go.
