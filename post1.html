Starting Small

April 12, 2026

Since March of 2025 I have been living/practicing at the Buddhist Monastery named MAPLE: Monastic Academy for the Preservation of Life on Earth. Training at the Monastic Academy has forced me to face my lack of integrity, that lack being the cause to my suffering, and my responsibility to live with integrity and therefore happiness. Living in a place of training is very different than an occasional zoom call, meditation session on an app, or even sometimes going to a live meditation session. You learn, in the first moments of stepping down to the ground, this is real. My life has utterlly changed even though I am just a beginner.
